## How I approach delivery

1. Understand the business problem, users and desired outcome.
2. Clarify requirements, constraints and the scope of the solution.
3. Translate the scope into an executable plan and coordinate the right people.
4. Keep development, UX, testing and stakeholders aligned.
5. Validate the working solution, address gaps and support deployment or handover.

I value practical problem-solving, clear ownership, honest status reporting and evidence-based decisions over process for its own sake.
